Free search and filters: how should filtering look?

Checklist is noted for the wait. This is the free Search button: keywords and filters, no AI, no sign-in. Every number here is real: “carburetor icing” appears in 897 completed US investigations, 366 of them in the probable cause, and 58 of those in the initial climb.

Filters live as chips under the search box; “+ Filter” opens a small panel with counts. The calmest; the page stays one reading column like the briefing sheet.
