How complete is the recent record?

Investigations take time. 2025 has 758 completed of 1,176 events, and 2026 only 270 of 870, so recent years look smaller than they will be.

When in the flight did it happen?

The defining event was during landing in 7,849 cases (32%). That counts investigations, not risk per landing.

How serious were the outcomes?

3,918 investigations (16%) involved a fatality; more than half had no injuries.

What was the weather?

1,084 (4.5%) were in instrument conditions.

What was flying?

Mostly airplanes; 2,335 helicopters (9.6%).

Where?

Lower 48 shown; Alaska and Hawaii follow. 13 cases lack coordinates.
