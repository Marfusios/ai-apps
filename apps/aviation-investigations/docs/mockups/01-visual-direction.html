Shared in every direction (pilot-native meaning, not decoration)

Magenta = AI-derivedThe "magenta line" is what automation computes and pilots are trained to cross-check. Here, magenta always marks AI interpretation; NTSB text stays in plain ink.
Evidence glyphs● causal fault established · ◐ defect found, causal link not established · ○ no fault identified · dashed ring = insufficient evidence. Always shown with words, never colour alone.
Flight profileEach case's NTSB event sequence (phases plus the defining event) drawn like the profile view on an approach plate. Built from real data, not illustration.
Real conditionsVMC/IMC, light and wind, plus the raw METAR string where NTSB recorded one (about 1 in 4 US cases).
